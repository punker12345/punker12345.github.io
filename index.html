About

I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Law, City University of Hong Kong. My research examines how law and public policy can govern artificial intelligence.

My work sits at the intersection of technology regulation, administrative law, and public policy. I study how governments build capacity to oversee automated systems, how regulatory institutions adapt to rapidly changing technology, and what accountability should look like when public and private actors deploy AI at scale.

Before joining City University of Hong Kong, I completed my doctoral studies in law and held research positions focused on the regulation of emerging technologies. I teach in the areas of technology law, administrative law, and legal methods.
