Teaching

I teach courses on technology regulation, administrative law, and legal research and writing at both the undergraduate (LLB / JD) and postgraduate (LLM) levels.

Current courses

  • Law and Regulation of Artificial Intelligence

    LLM seminar · Semester A, 2026–27

    A seminar on the legal frameworks that govern AI development and deployment: risk-based regulation, liability, data protection, transparency obligations, and the comparative landscape across the EU, China, and common-law jurisdictions.

  • Administrative Law

    LLB / JD core course · Semester B, 2026–27

    Judicial review, the grounds of challenge to administrative action, procedural fairness, and the institutions of the regulatory state, with attention to how automated decision-making tests established doctrine.
