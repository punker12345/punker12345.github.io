Research

My research asks how legal and policy institutions can hold AI systems accountable without stifling their public value.

Three themes run through my work: regulatory capacity — whether agencies have the people, information, and authority to supervise automated systems; institutional design — how oversight bodies, courts, and legislatures divide the work of governing technology; and comparative governance — what jurisdictions in Asia, Europe, and North America can learn from one another.
